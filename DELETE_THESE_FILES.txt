Delete these files from your Laravel project (they are replaced by
app/Http/Middleware/EnsureUserHasRole.php and the new tests):

app/Http/Middleware/AdminMiddleware.php
app/Http/Middleware/CafeOwnerMiddleware.php
app/Http/Middleware/UserMiddleware.php
tests/Feature/ExampleTest.php
tests/Unit/ExampleTest.php

Then run, from the project folder:

  php artisan migrate          (adds indexes + fixes timer goal status - your data is kept)
  php artisan config:clear
  php artisan db:seed          (adds badges + the admin account; safe to re-run)
  php artisan test             (should show 41 passed)

Add these lines to your .env (see .env.example for what each one does):

  SANCTUM_EXPIRATION_MINUTES=43200
  ADMIN_EMAIL=admin@cafeverse.test
  ADMIN_PASSWORD=choose-a-strong-password
  CAFEVERSE_MIN_VISIT_MINUTES=5
  CAFEVERSE_ONE_VISIT_PER_DAY=true
